About Us

The Ateneo Computer Engineering Society (ACES) is a student organization of Ateneo de Zamboanga University composed of Computer Engineering students. ACES promotes technical excellence, innovation, and leadership through projects, trainings, and community service. In collaboration with groups like the NDRRMC, ACES uses technology to serve and create positive change for society.
